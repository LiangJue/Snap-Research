# Domain Glossary

- **Research Report**: One captured source (a screenshot region or selected text) and the collection of Candidate Claims, Research Tasks, and Evidence Cards created from it.
- **Candidate Claim**: One researchable scenario extracted from the captured source before research begins. Tightly coupled verification points remain together.
- **Research Task**: The investigation of exactly one selected Candidate Claim and all tightly coupled verification points inside it.
- **Evidence Card**: The result and user judgment for exactly one Research Task.
- **Claim Selection**: The user-confirmed subset of Candidate Claims that should become Research Tasks.
- **Text Selection Capture**: A captured browser text selection that bypasses screenshot creation and image recognition.
